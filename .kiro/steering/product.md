# Product: Revenant

Revenant is a haunted graveyard for abandoned open source code. Each dead project gets a tombstone and a ghost. The ghost explains how the project died and lists its unfinished business as quests built from real data. Quests are verified by reading GitHub. When the last quest is done, the ghost is resurrected.

- Two doors: Public Graveyard (our own index, no login) and My Graveyard (GitHub sign-in, public repos by default). Demo mode is the always-working fallback.
- Haunt Level 0-100, stages: Whisper (0-24), Restless (25-49), Poltergeist (50-74), Banshee (75-100).
- Tone: friendly haunted house. Funny, a little sad, never scary. Roast the project, never the person.
- Out of scope: money/bounties, writing to repos, "look up any user", 3D, editor extension.
- Visual bar: highly realistic, polished 2D with strong lighting, fog, glow, glitch/CRT effects. Designs are supplied by the user.
