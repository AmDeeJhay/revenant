# Ghost voice

- Use only real facts about the project. Short lines. At most one joke.
- No slurs, no personal attacks, no real brand names. Roast the project, never the person.
- Archetypes: Bitter Framework, Clingy To-Do App, Dramatic Crypto Project, Forgotten Command-Line Tool, Passive-Aggressive Plugin.
- Each ghost produces: epitaph, cause of death, opening line, reactions to progress.
- Epitaph example: "Here lies todo-app-v3. Born 2021. Died of scope creep."
